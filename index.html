About

Current directions

Ongoing research

01

Multi-Step Proximal Policy Improvement

Investigating re-centered multi-step policy refinement and its stability across policy-improvement budgets, refinement depths, and critic conditions.

02

Adaptive Policy Optimization

Developing adaptive refinement of step size and depth, together with value-guided interpolation between behavior-supported student and multi-step teacher policies.

03

Extensions of PathBridger

Exploring offline-to-online learning, action-free settings, and more flexible generative approaches to long-horizon goal-conditioned control.

Policy Optimization in Offline RL

Behavior-regularized updates as proximal steps, multi-step refinement, policy geometry, and learning under imperfect critic estimates.

Generative Models for Decision Making

Sequence, diffusion, energy-based, and flow-based models for trajectory generation, planning, and long-horizon control.
