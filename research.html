Recent Work

PathBridger: Subgoal Bridges for Offline Goal-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning

Soohyun Choi*, Seonvin Cho*, Songnam Hong · Under Review · *Equal contribution

  • Developed a hierarchical offline GCRL framework that connects subgoal selection to short-horizon execution through state-space bridges
  • Combined generative endpoint proposals, transitive value-based selection, and inverse-dynamics decoding to produce executable action chunks from offline trajectories

Ongoing Work

Multi-Step Proximal Policy Improvement

  • Investigating how re-centered multi-step policy refinement can decouple critic bootstrap exposure from final policy improvement
  • Studying the stability of proximal refinement across policy-improvement budgets, refinement depths, and critic conditions

Adaptive Policy Optimization

  • Developing adaptive policy refinement that jointly adjusts refinement depth and step size based on critic reliability and realized policy movement
  • Investigating value-guided interpolation between behavior-supported student and multi-step teacher policies to control the extent of policy improvement from behavior-supported policies

Extensions of PathBridger

  • Exploring follow-up directions for long-horizon goal-conditioned decision making beyond the fully offline setting
  • Interested in extending state-space planning and generative approaches toward offline-to-online learning, action-free settings, and more flexible long-horizon control

Research Topics

Policy Optimization in Offline Reinforcement Learning

  • Interpreted behavior-regularized policy updates as proximal steps along a critic-induced gradient flow
  • Investigated multi-step policy refinement based on this view under different policy geometries and imperfect critic estimates

Generative Models for Sequential Decision Making

  • Studied a range of generative approaches to sequential decision making, including sequence models, diffusion models, energy-based methods, and flow-based models
  • Explored their use for trajectory generation, planning, and long-horizon control
